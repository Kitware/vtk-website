# History of VTK

VTK has a rich history spanning over three decades of development in scientific visualization.

## Origins (1993)

VTK was originally developed at GE Corporate Research & Development (now GE Global Research) in Niskayuna, New York. The project was created by Will Schroeder, Ken Martin, and Bill Lorensen as a companion to their textbook *The Visualization Toolkit: An Object-Oriented Approach to 3D Graphics*.

## Kitware and Open Source

- **1998** — Kitware, Inc. was founded to support and continue VTK development
- The project transitioned to a fully open-source development model
- Kitware became the primary steward of the VTK codebase

## Evolution

- Growth from a textbook companion to a comprehensive visualization framework
- Addition of parallel processing, volume rendering, and information visualization capabilities
- Development of language bindings for Python and Java
- Integration with major scientific computing ecosystems
- Spawned derivative projects including ParaView, 3D Slicer, and vtk.js

## VTK Today

VTK continues to evolve with active development, regular releases, and a growing community of contributors from academia, government laboratories, and industry worldwide.
