# Contributors

VTK is developed and maintained through the collaborative efforts of individuals and organizations worldwide.

## Kitware

[Kitware, Inc.](https://www.kitware.com) is the primary steward of VTK, providing core development, release management, and long-term maintenance. Kitware was founded in 1998 specifically to support VTK and other open-source scientific computing projects.

## National Laboratories

VTK has benefited from significant contributions by national laboratories, including:

- **Sandia National Laboratories** — Information visualization, parallel processing
- **Los Alamos National Laboratory** — Large-scale visualization, parallel rendering
- **Argonne National Laboratory** — Scientific visualization research

## Academic Institutions

Researchers and students from universities around the world have contributed algorithms, bug fixes, and extensions to VTK.

## Community Contributors

The VTK community includes hundreds of individual contributors who participate through:

- Code contributions via merge requests
- Bug reports and feature requests
- Documentation improvements
- Community support on the Discourse forum
- Conference presentations and tutorials

Interested in contributing? See our [Contributing Guide](/community/contributing) for details on how to get involved.
