# Engineering

VTK provides powerful post-processing and visualization capabilities for engineering simulation data.

## Capabilities

- **FEA visualization** — Stress, strain, displacement, and deformation
- **CFD post-processing** — Flow fields, streamlines, particle tracing
- **Mesh visualization** — Inspect and validate simulation meshes
- **Time series animation** — Animate transient simulation results
- **Derived quantities** — Compute gradients, vorticity, and other derived fields

## Supported Formats

- Exodus (Sandia National Laboratories)
- CGNS (CFD General Notation System)
- EnSight (Ansys)
- Fluent case/data files
- OpenFOAM
- Abaqus output databases

## Applications

- Structural analysis visualization
- Fluid dynamics post-processing
- Thermal analysis
- Electromagnetics visualization
- Multi-physics simulation results
