# Solutions

VTK powers visualization applications across a wide range of domains. Its flexible architecture makes it adaptable to virtually any field that works with spatial or scientific data.

## Domain Solutions

- [**Medical Imaging**](/solutions/medical) — DICOM, segmentation, surgical planning, 3D Slicer
- [**Engineering**](/solutions/engineering) — FEA/CFD post-processing, structural analysis
- [**Geospatial**](/solutions/geospatial) — GIS, terrain visualization, earth science
- [**Scientific**](/solutions/scientific) — General-purpose scientific visualization
- [**Web Visualization**](/solutions/web) — Browser-based visualization with vtk.js, Trame, and WebAssembly
