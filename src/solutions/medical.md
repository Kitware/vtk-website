# Medical Imaging

VTK is widely used in medical imaging for visualization, analysis, and surgical planning.

## Capabilities

- **DICOM support** — Read and process medical imaging data
- **Volume rendering** — Visualize CT, MRI, and other volumetric scans
- **Surface extraction** — Generate 3D models from image data (marching cubes)
- **Image segmentation** — Label and separate anatomical structures
- **Multi-planar reformatting** — Axial, sagittal, and coronal views
- **Measurement tools** — Distance, angle, and volume measurements

## Applications

- Surgical planning and simulation
- Radiation therapy planning
- Medical device design
- Anatomical education and training
- Clinical research and trials

## Related Projects

- [**3D Slicer**](/ecosystem/slicer) — Open-source medical imaging platform built on VTK
- **ITK** — Insight Toolkit for image segmentation and registration
- **MONAI** — Medical Open Network for AI
