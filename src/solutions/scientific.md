# Scientific Visualization

VTK is the foundation for scientific visualization across physics, chemistry, biology, and other research domains.

## Capabilities

- **Scalar visualization** — Color mapping, contouring, isosurfaces
- **Vector visualization** — Glyphs, streamlines, stream surfaces
- **Tensor visualization** — Ellipsoid glyphs, hyperstreamlines
- **Volume visualization** — Direct volume rendering of 3D scalar fields
- **Time-varying data** — Animate and explore temporal datasets
- **Multi-dimensional data** — Parallel coordinates, scatter plots

## Research Domains

- **Physics** — Simulation results, particle physics detectors
- **Chemistry** — Molecular visualization, electron density maps
- **Biology** — Microscopy data, protein structures
- **Astronomy** — Cosmological simulations, survey data
- **Materials science** — Microstructure analysis, crystal structures

## Large-Scale Data

- Parallel processing with MPI for HPC environments
- In-situ visualization during simulation runs (via Catalyst)
- Streaming for out-of-core datasets
- Adaptive resolution with multi-resolution data structures
