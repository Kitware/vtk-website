# Community

VTK has a vibrant community of developers, researchers, and users worldwide.

## Get Help

- [**VTK Discourse Forum**](https://discourse.vtk.org) — Ask questions, share knowledge, and discuss VTK
- [**Examples**](https://examples.vtk.org) — Searchable collection of working code examples
- [**Stack Overflow**](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/tagged/vtk) — Questions tagged with `vtk`

## Contribute

- [**Contributing Guide**](/community/contributing) — How to contribute code, docs, and bug reports
- [**VTK GitLab**](https://gitlab.kitware.com/vtk/vtk) — Source code, issues, and merge requests

## Professional Support

- [**Kitware Services**](/community/services) — Professional development, training, and consulting
- [**Events**](/community/events) — Office hours, workshops, and conferences
