# Events

Stay connected with the VTK community through regular events.

## Office Hours

Kitware hosts regular VTK and ParaView office hours where community members can ask questions and get help from core developers.

- Open to all community members
- Informal Q&A format
- Announcements posted on [Discourse](https://discourse.vtk.org)

## Conferences

### VIS (IEEE Visualization Conference)
The premier forum for visualization research. VTK-related papers and tutorials are regularly presented.

### Kitware Annual Webinars
Technical webinars covering VTK features, best practices, and case studies.
